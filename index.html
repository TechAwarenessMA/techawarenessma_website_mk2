What we do

Three ways in.

01

Weekly Repair Workshops

Drop in, pick up a driver, open something. A 12-week curriculum you can join any week — no signup needed to attend.

See how it works →
02

Senior Tech Support

One-on-one help at Southgate Senior Center. Your device, your questions, your pace. No judgment, ever.

See how it works →
03

E-Waste Drives

Bring us your dead electronics. We triage every device: resell, refurbish, parts, or recycle. Nothing goes to waste by default.
